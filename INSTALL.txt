asmtone 2.1 — send text from one Mac to another as sound
========================================================

You need: a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4 ...) running macOS 12 Monterey or later.
Intel Macs are not supported.


INSTALL
-------

1. Double-click asmtone-2.1-mac-arm64.zip to unzip it (Safari may already have done this).

2. Drag asmtone.app into your Applications folder.

3. Open it the first time. asmtone isn't from the App Store, so macOS checks with you first:

   macOS 15 Sequoia and later
     a. Double-click asmtone in Applications. macOS says it "Not Opened". Click Done.
     b. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to
        "asmtone was blocked to protect your Mac", and click Open Anyway.
     c. Enter your password if asked, then click Open Anyway again.

   macOS 12 - 14
     Right-click (or Control-click) asmtone in Applications, choose Open, then click Open.

   You only do this once. After that asmtone opens like any other app.

   (Terminal alternative, any macOS:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/asmtone.app )

4. When asmtone asks to use the microphone, click Allow. It needs the microphone to hear
   messages; nothing is recorded or sent anywhere. If you clicked Don't Allow by mistake:
   System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone > turn on asmtone.

5. Optional: to keep it in the Dock, right-click its Dock icon > Options > Keep in Dock.


USE
---

- Install asmtone on two Macs in the same room, with Listen on (it is on by default).
  Both Macs need version 2.x: it plays differently from 1.x, so the two don't understand
  each other.
- On one Mac, pick Piano, Chirp, Fast or Silent, type a message and press Return.
  The other Mac writes it down.

     Piano   piano notes                   about 3 characters a second
     Chirp   short bird-like chirps        about 14 characters a second
     Fast    four tones at once            about 50 characters a second
     Silent  17.6-19.6 kHz, too high for   about 12 characters a second
             most adults to hear (children and dogs may)

  The receiver hears all four at once; you only choose a mode when sending.
  One message holds up to about 240 characters (fewer for emoji and accents).

- Menu bar (piano keys icon): Send Clipboard as Piano / Chirp / Fast / Silent, Listen,
  Copy Last Message, Save Last Minute of Sound, Show Last Failed Message.
  While a message is arriving the icon shows how far along it is. It dims when not listening.
- Dock icon: right-click for the same send items. A badge counts messages heard while you were
  in another app.

Tips
- Turn the volume up to about half or more.
- Distance: Piano, Chirp and Silent work across a room (they cope with the room's echo);
  Fast needs the Macs within a few feet of each other.
- A Mac doesn't hear its own messages (it stops listening while it plays).
- "N bytes repaired" means some sound was damaged and asmtone fixed it.
  A red line means a message was too damaged to fix: send it again, or try another mode.
- If a message fails, asmtone keeps its sound: menu bar > Show Last Failed Message shows the
  file (last-failed-message.wav) so you can send it to whoever is helping you.
- "Too loud: the microphone is overloading" means the Macs are too close or too loud:
  turn the sending Mac's volume down a little.
- Silent needs speakers and microphones that reach 19 kHz; most Macs do. If Silent never
  arrives, turn the volume up, or move the Macs closer.


UNINSTALL
---------

Quit asmtone (menu bar icon > Quit asmtone) and drag it from Applications to the Trash.
